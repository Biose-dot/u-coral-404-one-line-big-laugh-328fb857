# One Line, Big Laugh

One short line: the standing desk was bought for health, and it worked — the snacking just moved uphill.

> I bought a standing desk to get healthier — now I just stand still and snack at a higher altitude.

`main.py` copies the committed line in `assets/entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
